## SLIDE 7 — WHAT IS D&D?

**Title:** D&D in One Paragraph

**Body:**
Dungeons & Dragons is a collaborative storytelling game. One person (the Dungeon Master, that's me) sets the scene. Everyone else plays a character and decides what they do. You roll dice to find out how it goes. There are no winners, no losers — just the story you all make together.

**Callout:**
> Think of it less like a board game and more like improv theater...
> with math.

---

## SLIDE 8 — THE GAMEPLAY LOOP

**Title:** How a Session Actually Works

**Step 1 — The DM describes the scene:**
"You enter a dimly lit tavern. A hooded figure watches you from the corner."

**Step 2 — Players decide what to do:**
"I walk over and introduce myself." / "I steal their drink." / "I cast Detect Magic."

**Step 3 — Roll dice if there's risk:**
The dice decide how well it goes. Not if it happens — how.

**Step 4 — Story continues:**
Whatever happens, the story keeps moving. Yes-and energy!

## SLIDE 10 — YOUR CHARACTER

**Title:** What Is a Character?

**Body:**
Your character is your avatar in the world. They have:
- **A Race/Species** — What they are (elf, human, tiefling, etc.)
- **A Class** — What they do (wizard, fighter, rogue, etc.)
- **Stats** — How good they are at things (Strength, Dexterity, etc.)
- **A Backstory** — Who they are and why they're here

**Tip for new players:**
> Don't overthink it. Pick something that sounds fun.
> You can't make a "wrong" character.

## SLIDE 11 — THE DM vs. THE PLAYERS

**Title:** Roles at the Table

**DM (That's Me):**
- Runs the world, NPCs, and monsters
- Sets the scene, adjudicates rules
- Is rutting for the players
- Helps facilitate a fun environment for everyone

**Players (That's You):**
- Play your character honestly
- Drive the story forward
- Collaborate, don't compete as players and characters
- Ask questions — there are no dumb ones
- Helps facilitate a fun environment

**Shared:**

## SLIDE 13 — THE PHONE SITUATION

**Title:** Phones 📵

**Body:**
Look, we're all adults. But here's the deal:

- **During active scenes:** phone face-down or away
- **Between scenes / on breaks:** totally fine
- **If it's urgent:** just step out, no explanation needed — life happens
- **Taking notes on your phone:** cool, just say so

**Callout:**
> The dragon does not care about your notifications.
> The dragon is, frankly, offended.

---

## SLIDE 14 — SCHEDULING & ABSENCES

**Title:** Showing Up (and When You Can't)

**Session schedule:**
> Every other Friday at 6pm

**If you can't make it:**
- Give as much notice as possible (24 hrs+ ideal)
- We'll still play if we have enough players
- Your character gets "conveniently busy" that session — no judgment
- The more consistant you are here the more "Screen Time" your character gets

**The rule:**
> Real life always comes first. Just communicate and we'll figure it out.

---

## SLIDE 15 — SAFETY TOOLS

**Title:** Keeping It Fun for Everyone

**The X Card:**
If something comes up in the story that makes you uncomfortable, just say **"X"** (or tap the table). No explanation needed. We skip it, no questions asked.

**Lines & Veils (quick version):**
- **Lines** = things we won't do at all *(tell me before/between sessions)*
- **Veils** = things that happen "off-screen" if they come up

**Open door policy:**
> If something isn't working for you — tone, a plot element, another player's behavior — come to me. You don't have to white-knuckle it.

**What we're aiming for:**
Uncomfortable in a *good, dramatic* way. Not uncomfortable in a *"I don't want to be here"* way.
