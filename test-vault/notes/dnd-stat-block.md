## Spell Description

### Fireball
*3rd-level evocation*

**Casting Time:** 1 action
**Range:** 150 feet
**Components:** V, S, M (a tiny ball of bat guano and sulfur)
**Duration:** Instantaneous

A bright streak flashes from your pointing finger to a point you choose within range and then blossoms with a low roar into an explosion of flame. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on that point must make a Dexterity saving throw.

## Two-Column Content Candidate

The following content is intended to demonstrate the two-column D&D layout (implemented in Issue #46):

### Monsters of the Darkwood

**Blightbat** — These disease-carrying creatures roost in the upper canopy of the Darkwood and hunt at dusk. Their bite carries a slow-acting fever that drains Constitution over several days.

**Thornhulk** — A massive plant creature that uproots itself to defend its territory. The Thornhulk's body is covered in razor-sharp thorns that damage attackers who strike it with unarmed or natural weapon attacks.

**Mirewalker** — An undead spirit that haunts the bogs at the forest's edge. It appears as a pale humanoid figure just below the water's surface and attempts to lure the living to their drowning deaths.

**Ashwolf** — A wolf infused with necrotic energy. Its grey-white coat seems to absorb light, and its howl causes nearby animals to flee in terror. A pack of Ashwolves is one of the most feared encounters in the Darkwood.
